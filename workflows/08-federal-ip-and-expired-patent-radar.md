# Workflow 08 — Federal IP Deal Flow & Expired-Patent Radar

## Objective

Exploit a lawful information gap around intellectual property by monitoring three public feeds:

1. patents/patent applications explicitly advertised for **license or sale**;
2. federal-laboratory technologies available for commercial licensing; and
3. patents whose rights have **lapsed/expired**, subject to status and reinstatement checks.

The value is commercialization intelligence, buyer/seller matching, or product-opportunity research — never pretending to own an invention merely because it appears in a public feed.

## Feed A — USPTO "Available for License or Sale"

The USPTO Official Gazette periodically publishes a section titled `Patents and Patent Applications Available for License or Sale`, with patent/application identifiers and owner contact information. This is unusually clean deal flow because the rightsholder is affirmatively advertising transaction interest.

### Monetization options

- contract with owner as commercialization scout / nonexclusive referral source;
- identify likely industry licensees and introduce them under a written success-fee agreement;
- sell a filtered technology-deal newsletter to corporate innovation teams;
- build sector-specific license/sale alerts.

Obtain a written agreement before expecting a commission. IP transactions should be documented by qualified counsel.

## Feed B — Federal Technology Transfer

The Federal Laboratory Consortium's FLC Business is a public search tool for technologies available for licensing, patents, facilities, researchers, funding and notices of intent to license. NASA separately exposes a Technology Transfer API and a portfolio of more than 1,200 licensable technologies.

NASA's current licensing page states that eligible U.S. startups can receive special commercial-license terms with **no up-front license fee**, while commercial running royalties are negotiated. This is not free ownership; it is a commercialization path.

### High-value sub-feed — Intent to grant exclusive licenses

FLC Business publishes notices of intent to grant exclusive/co-exclusive/partially-exclusive federal patent licenses. A commercial intelligence product can alert competing or adjacent companies while the opportunity is still public and actionable.

## Feed C — Patents Expiring for Failure to Pay Maintenance Fees

USPTO publishes weekly Official Gazette lists of patents that expired because required maintenance fees were not paid. USPTO states that the patent protection lapses and the patent rights are no longer enforceable when the required fee and surcharge are not timely paid.

### Critical caution

Some expired patents can later be reinstated through accepted late-maintenance-fee petitions. Also, a product may still be covered by other patents, continuations, foreign rights, regulatory restrictions, copyright, trade secrets or trademarks. Therefore a record is a **technology opportunity lead**, not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Scoring model

- market category and buyer count
- age / remaining commercial relevance
- explicit license/sale interest
- federal/startup-friendly license availability
- competing patents / family complexity
- prototype/readiness evidence
- ease of independently manufacturing/implementing
- regulated-industry burden
- owner contactability
- potential buyer fit

## Fast MVP

Produce one weekly "Underused IP Radar" with:

- 10 owner-advertised patents for license/sale;
- 10 federal technologies available to startups;
- 10 newly lapsed patents with plausible modern applications;
- 3 recommended buyer/licensee matches per selected item.

Sell the curated intelligence first. Do not acquire an expensive license until a commercialization case exists.
