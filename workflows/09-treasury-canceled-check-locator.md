# Workflow 09 — Treasury Canceled/Unpaid Check Research

1. Obtain Treasury/check-cancellation data lawfully, including through FOIA where appropriate.
2. Import with `fia ingest treasury-checks-file`.
3. Treat check symbol, number and amount as a payment signal only.
4. Identify the certifying federal agency from official records.
5. Ask the agency to verify whether the payment remains unpaid and identify the lawful payee through the agency's permitted process.
6. Before compensated locating/outreach, review the law governing the contemplated locator activity and the payee's jurisdiction.
7. Obtain a compliant owner/payee agreement before recovery representation where required.
8. Never claim the check, redirect payment, or represent FIA as the payee.
