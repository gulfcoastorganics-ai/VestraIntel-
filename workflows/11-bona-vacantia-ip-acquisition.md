# Workflow 11 — Dissolved-Company IP Acquisition Review

1. Detect a dissolved company through Companies House.
2. Resolve independent patent/trademark/IP records to the entity.
3. Verify that the company owned the asset at dissolution.
4. Check whether the company has been restored and whether title has already moved.
5. Determine which Crown body has jurisdiction over the asset.
6. Obtain an independent commercial valuation and freedom-to-operate review as appropriate.
7. Refer the asset through the official bona vacantia process.
8. Do not use, license, sell or represent ownership of the asset until a valid transfer/license has completed.
