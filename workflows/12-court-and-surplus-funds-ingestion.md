# Workflow 12 — Court-Held and Surplus Funds

## Bankruptcy

- Use official court records/exports; do not automate around CAPTCHA.
- Preserve debtor, creditor, case number, court and amount provenance.
- Research original entitlement and any successor/assignment chain.
- Route filings to human/court-specific review.

## County/court surplus

- Import only with explicit jurisdiction, custodian and official source URL.
- Identify the apparent former owner/claimant and case/sale reference.
- Check statutory priority, claim deadline, finder/locator restrictions, assignment rules and fee caps for that jurisdiction.
- Do not assume a surplus becomes the discoverer's property or that a rule from another state/county applies.
