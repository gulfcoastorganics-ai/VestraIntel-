# Action gates

| Gate | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `public_intelligence_only` | May ingest, normalize, score and sell derived analysis. Does not authorize a claim, legal conclusion, objection, assignment or owner contact. |
| `owner_only` | The underlying property belongs to an owner/heir/rightsholder. Discovery does not create ownership. |
| `licensed_only` | Direct locator/representative activity is held until required registration/licensing and contract rules are satisfied. |
| `review_required` | No action until the custodian's current procedure and governing jurisdiction are verified. |

The software must never convert a record into a claim submission automatically.
