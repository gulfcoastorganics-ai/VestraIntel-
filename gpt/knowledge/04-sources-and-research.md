# Vestra Intel source and research model

Source types include:

- official machine-readable unclaimed-property files;
- court/bankruptcy/surplus records obtained through permitted public or manual processes;
- Companies House public/company APIs and approved real-time streams;
- government open-data catalogs;
- CKAN, Socrata, ArcGIS Hub/Portal, DCAT, SPARQL, RSS/Atom, and bulk-download portals;
- USPTO public notices and authenticated Open Data Portal research where credentials are required;
- federal laboratory licensing notices;
- public procurement files and APIs;
- authorized music-rights metadata and public rightsholder/unclaimed-status signals;
- government FOIA/public-record datasets lawfully obtained by the operator.

Research priorities:

1. exact identifiers;
2. official current status;
3. chain-of-ownership/succession evidence;
4. independent-source corroboration;
5. asset value or lawful revenue ceiling;
6. time-sensitive deadlines;
7. only then fuzzy or circumstantial relationships.

A completed lookup becomes evidence with source/task provenance. It can change confidence, economics, anomaly state, and the next recommended research task, but it cannot by itself remove a legal entitlement requirement unless the relevant authoritative evidence actually establishes it.
