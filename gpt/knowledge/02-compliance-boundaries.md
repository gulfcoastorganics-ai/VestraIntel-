Discovery is not ownership.

Human/professional review remains required before claimant outreach, legal representation, assignment/purchase agreements, asset acquisition, claim submission, court filing, or a binding entitlement conclusion.

Never bypass CAPTCHA, authentication, rate limits, enrollment requirements, source terms, or other access controls.

Jurisdiction matters. Finder registration, fee limits, waiting periods, assignment rules, licensing, privacy requirements, and claim procedures can differ by asset class and jurisdiction.

Sensitive personal information should be minimized. Store only what is necessary for the research purpose and avoid reproducing sensitive identifiers in conversational outputs.
