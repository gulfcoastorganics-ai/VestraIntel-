# Vestra Intel monetization and economics

Primary revenue lanes:

- locator_fee: reconnect an entitled owner with an asset under a compliant agreement where permitted;
- successor_assignment_review: investigate whether a documented assignment, purchase, merger, acquisition, or succession path may create a lawful claimant;
- asset_acquisition: investigate assets that can lawfully be purchased through an official owner/custodian process;
- intelligence_sale: sell derived public-data intelligence, reconciliation, monitoring, or lead research without claiming the underlying asset;
- licensing_introduction: identify licensors/licensees and create a compliant introduction or intelligence product;
- owner_only: the value belongs to the qualifying owner/rightsholder; Vestra can provide research/intelligence but should not treat the asset as available for acquisition.

Economic terms must stay separate:

face value != fee ceiling != expected case value != realized revenue != profit.

FIA's expected-case-value is a decision-support estimate that may discount a lawful revenue reference by evidence confidence, probability of viability, time, regulatory friction, and research cost. It is not a promise or forecast of payment.
