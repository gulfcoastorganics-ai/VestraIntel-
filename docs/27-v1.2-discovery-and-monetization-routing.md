# v1.2 — Discovery Breadth and Monetization Routing

Date: 2026-08-14

## Objective

v1.2 broadens FIA beyond state unclaimed property and corporate/IP feeds while keeping the core rule intact: discovery is not ownership. The new layer assigns each opportunity/anomaly to a lawful commercial route and records the conditions that must be satisfied before external action.

## Monetization route taxonomy

| Route | Revenue model | Typical FIA signal | Core gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `locator_fee` | disclosed fee after a valid owner agreement/recovery | state property, Treasury canceled-check research, jurisdiction-reviewed surplus | owner/payee authority + applicable locator law |
| `successor_assignment_review` | lawful assignment/acquisition/successor recovery if permitted | bankruptcy funds, dissolved-creditor cases | documented chain of ownership + court/jurisdiction review |
| `asset_acquisition` | buy asset through official process, then use/license/resell | dissolved-company IP / bona vacantia | title + valuation + official transfer |
| `intelligence_sale` | B2B data, reconciliation, monitoring, research | MLC metadata, royalty mismatch, SAM opportunities, lapsed technology | lawful data access + accurate representation |
| `licensing_introduction` | contracted advisory/referral fee | FLC/USPTO licensing notices | current licensor authority + written commercial terms |
| `owner_only` | no direct asset monetization without a separately permitted service | SoundExchange unclaimed status, heir-only estates | lawful creator/owner/heir entitlement |

Routes are **triage outputs**, not legal conclusions. The `route_score` expresses how clearly the current evidence maps to a commercial model, not the probability of collecting money.

## New source modes

### SoundExchange status import

`fia ingest soundexchange-file <file>` imports a legitimately obtained public status list/export. Imported records remain `owner_only`. FIA may use the data as a reconciliation signal, but it will not route an unrelated operator to claim royalties.

### The MLC authorized-data import

`fia ingest mlc-file <file>` accepts a CSV/TSV/ZIP derived from authorized MLC data access. These records route to metadata/reconciliation intelligence. ISRCs are normalized for cross-source entity matching.

### Treasury canceled/unpaid check research

`fia ingest treasury-checks-file <file>` accepts records lawfully obtained from Treasury or a certifying agency, including FOIA results. Treasury cancellation-list rows are treated as *payment signals*, not named-payee proof. The route requires the issuing agency to confirm the lawful payee before any locator/recovery action.

### U.S. Bankruptcy Court funds

`fia ingest bankruptcy-file <file>` imports an official/manual court data export. FIA does not automate around the national locator CAPTCHA. Bankruptcy opportunities use the `successor_claim` legal model and remain chain-of-ownership/court-review gated.

### Official county/court surplus funds

`fia ingest surplus-file` requires explicit `--jurisdiction`, `--custodian`, and `--source-url` values. FIA intentionally has no national surplus fee/assignment rule; each imported record requires jurisdiction-specific priority, deadline, locator, and assignment review.

### SAM.gov public opportunities

`fia ingest sam-opportunities-file <file>` imports the public Contract Opportunities file extract for procurement timing/rebid intelligence. It is not an unclaimed-property source and does not imply bid eligibility.

## Cross-system examples

### Royalty reconciliation

```text
SoundExchange unclaimed-status signal
        + exact ISRC
MLC work / recording metadata
        ↓
royalty_metadata_mismatch anomaly
        ↓
intelligence_sale route
        ↓
metadata audit / rightsholder reconciliation service
```

The route explicitly prohibits claiming or redirecting third-party royalties.

### Dissolved-company IP

```text
Companies House dissolution
        +
patent/trademark/IP signal
        ↓
dissolved_company_ip anomaly
        ↓
asset_acquisition route
        ↓
verify ownership at dissolution
verify restoration/current title
obtain valuation
use official Crown/referral process where applicable
```

### Canceled federal check

```text
Treasury cancellation record
        ↓
check symbol + check number + amount
        ↓
identify certifying agency
        ↓
agency identifies lawful payee
        ↓
review locator rules applicable to contemplated service
        ↓
only then consider owner outreach/agreement
```

## Operating boundary

v1.2 still does not automate claimant outreach, owner agreements, assignments, asset purchases, court filings, royalty claims, or legal entitlement decisions. It expands discovery and tells an analyst **which business model is potentially available and what evidence/legal gates stand between the signal and that model**.
