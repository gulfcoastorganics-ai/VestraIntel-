# v0.8 Case Resolution Engine

v0.8 turns anomaly research into bounded case resolution.

A case has:

- a target research state;
- explicit research-resolvable conditions;
- non-automatable legal/compliance gates;
- a finite research budget;
- progress and evidence scores;
- expected-value-of-information (EVI) rankings for pending research;
- exactly one highest-EVI eligible next lookup.

`review_ready` means the configured research questions have been answered sufficiently to route the case to a human. It never means ownership, entitlement, authority to contact an owner, authority to purchase a claim, or authority to file.

## Expected value of information

The resolver combines:

- expected evidence uplift declared by the research planner;
- number of missing target conditions the task could answer;
- number of anomaly blockers the task was designed to investigate;
- anomaly severity and current actionability;
- whether the task adds a new independent source;
- execution/access cost;
- unresolved semantic prerequisites.

The score is a research-priority heuristic, not a financial return projection.

## Finite recursion

Completed research consumes budget units. Recursive graph expansion can keep discovering additional companies, officers, assignments, filings, or successor candidates, but v0.8 only selects work that fits inside the remaining case budget. This prevents open-ended graph crawling.

## Hard gates

Human/legal gates remain visible even when research progress reaches 100%. Examples include claimant entitlement, owner agreements, operator registration, court-specific successor review, rightsholder entitlement, title/freedom-to-operate review, and human approval before outreach or filing.
