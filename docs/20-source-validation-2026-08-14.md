# Source Validation — 2026-08-14

v0.8 continues to use authoritative public/official sources and keeps access gates intact.

- Companies House Public Data API exposes company profile, filing history, officers, PSC, insolvency, charges, and officer-appointment resources. API-key authorization is required for the relevant public-data endpoints.
- U.S. Courts states bankruptcy unclaimed funds are held for a person entitled to them; an owner, successor, or other claimant must prove the right to the funds. Individual bankruptcy courts may require documented assignment/purchase/merger/acquisition/succession chains.
- USPTO Open Data Portal requires a USPTO.gov account from June 18, 2026; v0.8 keeps patent research credential-gated.
- New York OSC caps location-service-provider fees at 15% and requires a signed, witnessed/notarized owner agreement; its quarterly data file omits amounts and taxpayer identifiers.
- California recognizes investigators/asset locators but caps covered fees at 10% of returned property and requires owner-facing disclosure/contract compliance.

No source-access restriction is bypassed by the case resolver.
