# v0.7 Recursive Evidence Ingestion

v0.7 closes the loop between research execution and the evidence graph.

## Flow

```text
anomaly
  -> ranked research task
  -> official/manual result
  -> normalized research facts
  -> graph entities and relations
  -> commercial re-score
  -> anomaly re-evaluation
  -> next research wave
```

Completed task results are not treated as ownership or entitlement by default. They are normalized into `research_facts` with source provenance, task provenance, confidence, and the exact evidence payload that produced each relation.

## Supported result assimilation

The first structured parsers cover Companies House:

- company profiles;
- filing histories;
- officers;
- persons with significant control;
- insolvency cases/practitioners; and
- officer appointment histories.

Manual/legal research can be attached through `fia task-result-file` using explicit structured facts. Manual assertions are capped at conservative confidence and marked `review_required`.

## Recursive expansion

A Companies House officer result can expose an official officer identifier. The planner can then request the officer's appointment history. If that history exposes another UK company number, the next planning pass schedules an official company-profile lookup for that company.

This is deliberately bounded. It does not crawl arbitrary social graphs and does not convert shared officers, addresses, or controllers into proof of common ownership.

## Persistence

Two tables were added:

- `research_result_ingestions`: one assimilation record per completed research task/result fingerprint;
- `research_facts`: normalized subject/relation/object evidence used to rebuild the graph.

Re-running assimilation is idempotent when the result fingerprint has not changed. If a completed result changes, its prior facts are replaced and the new facts are recorded.

## Commands

```bash
fia assimilate
fia facts --limit 100
fia feedback-runs
fia task-result-file 12 result.json
fia pipeline
```

`fia pipeline` now assimilates completed research results before rebuilding the graph and planning the next wave.

## Safety boundary

The feedback loop is evidence automation, not claim automation. It does not:

- assert legal ownership from corporate association;
- bypass CAPTCHAs or access controls;
- send outreach;
- sign assignments;
- file claims;
- purchase assets; or
- remove court/jurisdiction/legal-review gates.
