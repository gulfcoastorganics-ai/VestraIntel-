# v0.5 — Anomaly and Discrepancy Engine

The purpose of v0.5 is to find **relationships that are more valuable than any one source record** while preserving the distinction between a research lead and a legal entitlement.

## Detection model

The engine runs after ingestion, entity resolution, and commercial inference:

```text
public/authorized source records
        ↓
normalization + stable identifiers
        ↓
conservative entity resolution
        ↓
commercial inference
        ↓
compound anomaly rules
        ↓
ranked finding + provenance + blocks
        ↓
human review
```

A finding contains:

- rule/anomaly type
- linked entity and opportunity IDs
- independent source IDs
- confidence
- severity
- current commercial/actionability scores
- exact evidence records
- hard blocks
- recommended verification steps
- analyst state (`open`, `confirmed`, `dismissed`, `stale`)

## Initial rule catalog

### `dissolved_unclaimed_asset`

Requires a dissolved-company record and an unclaimed-funds/property record resolving to the same entity across at least two sources.

Interpretation: investigate successor, merger, restoration, shareholder, or other entitlement chains. It is never treated as proof that the operator owns the money.

### `dissolved_ip_signal`

Requires a dissolved-company record plus a distinct patent/IP signal.

Interpretation: investigate current title, assignment history, restoration, and—in applicable UK cases—whether an asset passed bona vacantia to the Crown.

### `bankruptcy_successor_signal`

Requires a dissolved entity plus a bankruptcy-held/successor-claim signal.

Interpretation: investigate the owner of record and documented assignment/purchase/merger/acquisition/succession chain. Local court procedures still control.

### `royalty_reconciliation_signal`

Requires independent royalty/rightsholder sources resolving to the same entity or recording.

Interpretation: metadata-quality or reconciliation intelligence. It does not authorize claiming somebody else's royalties.

### `public_domain_technology_signal`

Requires a USPTO maintenance-fee expiration plus a separate live technology/commercial signal.

Interpretation: potential technology-reuse research lead. Status must be rechecked because maintenance-fee expirations can sometimes be reinstated and other patent-family claims may remain.

### `cross_source_high_value_signal`

Requires at least three independent sources and a commercial score of at least 75.

Interpretation: prioritize manual review because breadth of independent evidence can reveal a relationship no individual dataset makes obvious.

### `high_value_identity_gap`

Flags a known face value of at least 10,000 currency units when the record still lacks independent identity corroboration.

Interpretation: spend research effort on identity resolution before outreach or filing.

### `changed_high_value_record`

Flags commercially significant source records that changed after their first ingestion.

Interpretation: re-verify value, owner, deadline, status, and entitlement assumptions before relying on stale analysis.

## Why rules are conservative

- Exact identifiers are stronger than names.
- Fuzzy organization matches are evidence, not merges.
- Fuzzy person-name merging remains disabled.
- A registered-office address is corroborating evidence, not shared ownership.
- Disappearance of a finding makes it `stale`; it is not deleted.
- Analyst confirmations/dismissals are retained for auditability.

## CLI

```bash
fia pipeline
fia anomaly-rules
fia anomalies --min-severity 70
fia anomalies --anomaly-type orphaned_business_asset
fia anomaly 12
fia anomaly-state 12 confirmed
```
