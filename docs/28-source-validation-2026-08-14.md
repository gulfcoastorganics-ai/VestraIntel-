# Source Validation — 2026-08-14

Only official/primary sources were used for the v1.2 source and action model.

## SoundExchange

Official creator/rightsholder page:
https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/

SoundExchange publicly describes four categories associated with unclaimed royalties: unregistered artists, partially unregistered artists, unregistered performers, and unregistered sound recording owners. Its guidance says creators/rightsholders must register/claim their own recordings to receive payments. FIA therefore imports these as owner/rightsholder signals, not transferable claims.

Key terms:
https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/key-terms/

## The Mechanical Licensing Collective (The MLC)

Official Data Programs:
https://www.themlc.com/dataprograms

The MLC provides Bulk Data Access and a Public Search API, and describes data programs intended for publishers, labels, metadata companies, music-technology companies and other industry participants. FIA keeps this source enrollment/authorization gated and uses it for reconciliation intelligence.

## Bureau of the Fiscal Service — canceled/unpaid federal checks

Official unclaimed-assets guidance:
https://fiscal.treasury.gov/about-us/unclaimed-assets

Fiscal Service states that locator businesses obtain federal check issuance/cancellation information under FOIA. It also states that Treasury cancellation listings contain check symbols, check numbers and dollar amounts and are not searchable by personal identifiers. Locator firms may combine those records with issuing-agency information to identify prospective payees. FIA therefore requires independent agency/payee confirmation before a Treasury signal can progress toward a locator workflow.

## U.S. Bankruptcy Courts

National judiciary guidance:
https://www.uscourts.gov/court-programs/bankruptcy/unclaimed-funds-bankruptcy

Unclaimed bankruptcy funds remain payable only to a party that establishes the right to the funds. FIA continues to prohibit CAPTCHA bypass and accepts only manually/officially obtained records for this source family.

## UK dissolved-company assets / bona vacantia

Overview:
https://www.gov.uk/claiming-money-or-property-from-dissolved-company/overview

Asset referral/purchase:
https://www.gov.uk/claiming-money-or-property-from-dissolved-company/buy-dissolved-company-assets

IP purchase guidance:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/buy-intellectual-property-bvc8

Official guidance confirms that dissolved-company assets may include intellectual property and that outsiders may refer certain assets for possible purchase. BVD checks title and normally disposes of IP at open-market value. FIA routes qualifying dissolved-company/IP anomalies to acquisition review, not automatic ownership.

## SAM.gov public Contract Opportunities

Official search/data services:
https://sam.gov/opportunities
https://sam.gov/data-services/Contract%20Opportunities/datagov?privacy=Public

SAM.gov provides public Contract Opportunities data and a public full CSV file extract. FIA uses this as procurement intelligence only; government contracting eligibility and bid submission remain separate workflows.

## Existing fee-cap controls revalidated

California State Controller investigator guidance:
https://sco.ca.gov/upd_investigators.html

California states that investigators/asset locators generally may not charge more than 10% of the property returned to owners.

New York OSC location-service-provider guidance:
https://www.osc.ny.gov/unclaimed-funds/resources/location-service-providers

New York states a 15% maximum fee and requires an owner agreement. Its provider data file excludes property amounts and taxpayer IDs.
